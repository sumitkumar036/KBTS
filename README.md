# 🚀 Class 12 Government Engineering Entrance Exams Tracker (B.Tech Admissions)

A comprehensive, curated tracker and informational roadmap for all major national and state-level government competitive exams conducted in India for admission into Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) and Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) programs post Class 12.

---

## 📌 Overview
Navigating the engineering admissions landscape in India after Class 12 can be overwhelming due to the sheer number of national and state-specific entrance examinations. This repository serves as a centralized dashboard to track eligibility, exam patterns, participating institutes, and official links for government-conducted engineering entrance tests.

## 🏆 National-Level Engineering Entrance Exams

| Exam Name | Conducting Body | Target Institutes | Key Highlights & Eligibility | Official Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **JEE Main** | National Testing Agency (NTA) | NITs, IIITs, CFTIs, & State-funded colleges | Cleared Class 12 with Physics & Mathematics. Basis for JEE Advanced eligibility. | [jeemain.nta.nic.in](https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/) |
| **JEE Advanced** | Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs) | All 23 IITs & IISERs, IIST, RGIPT | Must be in the top ~250,000 qualifiers of JEE Main. Maximum of 2 attempts in consecutive years. | [jeeadv.ac.in](https://jeeadv.ac.in/) |
| **CUET (UG)** | National Testing Agency (NTA) | Central & Renowned State Universities | Central Universities offering B.Tech track candidates based on specific Domain Subjects. | [exams.nta.ac.in/CUET-UG](https://exams.nta.ac.in/CUET-UG/) |

## 🗺️ State-Level Government CETs (Common Entrance Tests)

These examinations are conducted by state government boards/universities for admission into government engineering colleges and state-quota seats in private colleges.

| State | Exam Name | Conducting Authority | Top Participating Institutes | Official Link |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **West Bengal** | **WBJEE** | West Bengal Joint Entrance Examinations Board | Jadavpur University, Calcutta University | [wbjeeb.nic.in](https://wbjeeb.nic.in/) |
| **Maharashtra** | **MHT CET** | State Common Entrance Test Cell, Mumbai | COEP Tech Pune, VJTI Mumbai, ICT Mumbai | [cetcell.mahacet.org](https://cetcell.mahacet.org/) |
| **Karnataka** | **KCET** | Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) | UVCE Bengaluru, SGSITS, State Govt Colleges | [kea.kar.nic.in](https://kea.kar.nic.in/) |
| **Telangana** | **TG EAPCET** | JNTU Hyderabad on behalf of TSCHE | JNTU Colleges, OU College of Engineering | [eapcet.tsche.ac.in](https://eapcet.tsche.ac.in/) |
| **Andhra Pradesh** | **AP EAPCET** | JNTU Anantapur on behalf of APSCHE | AU College of Engineering, JNTUK | [cets.apsche.ap.gov.in](https://cets.apsche.ap.gov.in/) |

## 🛠️ General Syllabus Structure
Most competitive exams align directly with the **NCERT Class 11 and Class 12** curricula, focusing heavily on:
* **Physics:** Mechanics, Electrodynamics, Optics, Thermodynamics, Modern Physics.
* **Chemistry:** Physical Chemistry, Organic Reactions, Inorganic Trends, Coordination Compounds.
* **Mathematics:** Calculus, Coordinate Geometry, Vectors & 3D, Algebra, Matrices & Determinants.
